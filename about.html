About Lesotho

Discover the people, landscapes and heritage of the Mountain Kingdom.

The Kingdom in the Sky

Lesotho is a mountainous country in Southern Africa known for its high-altitude landscapes, traditional Basotho culture and distinctive natural scenery.

Tourism experiences can range from cultural and heritage visits to outdoor adventures, scenic drives, hiking and mountain activities.

Travel responsibly

Support local businesses, respect cultural practices, avoid littering and protect natural areas when travelling.

Travel Information

Useful topics to consider before exploring Lesotho.

Basotho culture is an important part of the country's tourism identity. Traditional clothing, music, crafts, food and historic sites offer opportunities for cultural learning.

Mountain weather can change quickly. Visitors should consider warm layers, comfortable footwear and weather-appropriate equipment for outdoor activities.

Plan routes carefully, use reputable service providers and follow local guidance, particularly in remote mountain areas.
